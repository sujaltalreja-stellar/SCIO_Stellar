========================================================================
             AETHERIS RENEWABLES - OPERATIONS CONTROL CENTER
========================================================================

Aetheris Renewables is a unified, context-aware Operational ERP platform 
designed to manage modern renewable energy portfolios (Solar, Wind, BESS).

This document outlines the core platform features in a simple, crisp format.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXECUTIVE PORTFOLIO & GIS OPERATIONS MAP
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Live Fleet Capacity: Aggregated MW generation vs. Total Capacity (GW) meter.
* SCADA Status Feed: Simulated real-time sensor loop updating every 5s.
* Spatial GIS View: Leaflet map showing plant locations with instant status overlays (Online, Offline, Maintenance).
* Local Weather telemetry: Live meteorological details (temperatures, wind speed, solar irradiance) per plant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ASSET REGISTRY & TELEMETRY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Component Catalog: Tracks inventory details of inverters, transformers, and battery racks.
* Health Indicators: Live asset health percentage scoring (0-100%) indicating active degradation.
* Deep Telemetry Parameters: Reads junction temperatures, battery state-of-charge, grid frequencies, and phase voltages.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. AI OPERATIONS COMMAND (MISTRAL AI INTEGRATED)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Context-Aware Chat: Operations Copilot reads plants, active alarms, cost center budgets, and work orders to answer queries.
* Predictive Anomalies: Dashboard flagging high-risk assets, Failure Probability (%), and Remaining Useful Life (RUL) days.
* Operation Recommendations: AI-generated preventive maintenance actions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FIELD OPERATIONS & SAFETY COMPLIANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Work Order Scheduler: Manages technician routing, scheduling, and workload balancing.
* Digital Checklist Inspections: Interactive digital checklists for field audits. Failed check-points automatically degrade asset health and log critical alarms.
* Digital Signatures: Touch signature submissions to lock completed safety checklists.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROCUREMENT & INVENTORY CONTROL
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* PR Approvals Manager: Purchase Requisition workflow showcasing cost estimates, required dates, and one-click approvals.
* Auto-Vendor Matching: POs are automatically routed to qualified supplier lists sorted by category specializations.
* Warehouse Bin Registry: Shelf bin locator mapping with safety-stock limit notifications.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. FINANCIAL CONTROL & PPA BILLING
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Cost Center allocation: Real-time budget consumption logs across Solar, Wind, and BESS segments.
* Invoice matching: Invoice checkouts linked back to purchase orders.
* PPA billing engine: Solar/Wind power generation integration with power rate agreements to calculate billings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. ENTERPRISE ERP SYNCHRONIZATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Connector status: Active visual feeds monitoring SAP, NetSuite, and Maximo links.
* Sync Logs: Chronological history of synced records.
* Automation Loops: Workflows that automatically update order queues and unlock dependencies upon parts arrival.
========================================================================
